How it works

Every 10 minutes a scheduled job saves the bike count of all ~3,400 YouBike stations in Taipei and New Taipei, plus the weather at 24 points. A machine-learning model (LightGBM) trained on this history predicts how many bikes each station will have one hour later.

1. CollectYouBike + weather, every 10 min
2. CleanTimestamps, quality flags, rebuildable
3. PredictChange in bikes over the next hour
4. ExplainTop reasons for each forecast

What the model looks at

  • Bikes and free docks right now, and how they changed in the last hour
  • The same time yesterday and one week ago
  • The 5 nearest stations within 1 km
  • Time of day, weekday or weekend, location
  • Current weather and the rain forecast for the next hour

"Why" on each station

For every station the model's forecast is split into the effect of each group of inputs, in bikes. A negative number means that group pushes the forecast down (fewer bikes in an hour).

Limits

Data collection started on 30 Oct 2026, so the model has seen only a few days, with little rain and no holidays. Forecasts are estimates, most useful for spotting stations that are about to run empty or full.
